1. Copy tpv.exe onto external media. Do not copy the repo.
2. On the examined host, from the USB (example E:):

     E:\tpv.exe collect --out E:\

   That writes HOSTNAME-YYYYMMDDTHHMMSSZ.tpv on the USB.
   --out FILE.tpv still works if you want a fixed name.
   Do not write onto C:\ unless you must. That needs
   --allow-local-write and it alters $MFT / $UsnJrnl.

   Progress prints on stderr. Not elevated is a warning, not a stop.
   Ctrl+C seals whatever was gathered (verify still works).

3. Still on USB, check before you leave:

     E:\tpv.exe info HOSTNAME-*.tpv
     E:\tpv.exe verify HOSTNAME-*.tpv

4. Take the .tpv and the .tpv.sha256 sidecar. On another PC,
   double-click TreePView.exe (or: TreePView.exe E:\case.tpv).
   Do not put TreePView.exe on the collection USB.

Rebuild on a development machine:

     powershell -File bin\install.ps1

Dev UI (analysis PC only):  powershell -File bin\install.ps1 -Dev
